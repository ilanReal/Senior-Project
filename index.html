Work Process

01
Brainstorming
I usually start by messing around with concepts and doing quick sketches to figure out the overall vibe.
02
Storyboarding
Once I have a solid idea, I map out the camera angles and figure out how the animation will actually flow.
03
Modeling & Sculpting
This is where the real work happens. I block out the basic shapes in Blender before diving into the fine details.
04
Lighting & Polish
I set up the lighting to lock in the mood, render it out, and use DaVinci Resolve to tie everything together.
