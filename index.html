How I Work

The Creative Process

Every animation follows the same four-stage pipeline — from rough idea to polished output.

01
💡
Concept
Sketching the core idea — what's the motion, what's the mood, what story does the geometry tell?
02
🧱
Modeling
Building forms in 3D space. Blockout first, then detail passes and topology refinement.
03
🎞
Animation
Keyframes, curves, and drivers. Rigging where needed. Getting the timing to feel alive.
04
✨
Render & Comp
Lighting passes, material tweaks, and final compositing in After Effects for color and grain.
